## Decision 001

### Title
Project Architecture

### Decision

The project will follow a modular architecture.

### Reason

A modular architecture improves readability, maintainability and scalability. New features such as AI, database support and plugins can be added without major refactoring.

## Decision 003

### Title

Dependency Management

### Decision

Runtime and development dependencies are separated.

### Reason

This keeps production environments lightweight and development environments complete.
